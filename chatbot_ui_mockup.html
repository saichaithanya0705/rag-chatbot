What are the different CPU scheduling algorithms?
CPU scheduling algorithms determine the order in which processes get CPU time. The main ones are FCFS, SJF, Round Robin, and Priority Scheduling. Round Robin is most commonly used in time-sharing systems — it assigns a fixed time quantum to each process in a circular queue.

Explain Round Robin in more detail with the time quantum concept.
In Round Robin scheduling, each process is given a fixed time slice called the time quantum (typically 10–100ms). If the process doesn't finish within its quantum, it's preempted and moved to the back of the ready queue. A smaller quantum improves responsiveness but increases context-switch overhead.
